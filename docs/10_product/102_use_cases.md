# Use Cases

## Corpus annotation and computational humanities

Use pattern IDs, work and unit IDs, evidence locators, annotator identity, and
version fields to prepare structured annotations. The structure can support
per-pattern agreement analysis and later import into annotation tools.

Preconditions: stable units, real cases, explicit evidence constraints, and a
tested annotation schema.

## Comparative media analysis

Compare how a literary pattern and a video pattern are realised without assuming
that the indicators are equivalent. Treat cross-media relations as hypotheses
until a study locates and adjudicates examples in both modalities.

Preconditions: parallel units, modality-specific evidence, and documented
analogue tests.

## Teaching

Use a pattern's definition, criteria, invented example, and creative exercise as
a self-contained lesson. Pair every invented example with a real located case
before using it for assessment or calibration.

Preconditions: a reviewed codebook and clear distinction between practice,
observation, and interpretation.

## Creative tooling

Use `creative_use` as a constraint in drafting and editorial review. For example,
a writer can plan an earlier cue and a later consequence rather than merely
label a scene as foreshadowing.

Preconditions: the creative prompt must preserve the pattern-specific evidence
requirements and not imply a guaranteed audience effect.

## LLM-assisted pre-annotation

Turn inclusion and exclusion criteria into a decision procedure and ask a model
to propose candidate annotations. A human must review evidence and adjudicate
uncertain or conflicting cases.

Preconditions: a stable schema, calibrated examples, false-positive cases,
prompt and model versions, and a measured comparison against human coding.

## Editorial and commissioning review

Ask which patterns a draft claims to use, which have located support, which are
absent after adequate inspection, and which remain uncertain. This can turn a
qualitative checklist into an auditable review.

Preconditions: an adequate inspected unit and agreement about whether absence
claims are within scope.

## Taxonomy research

Use original-workbook mappings and version history to document where a folk
taxonomy failed under coding pressure. This requires retained negative cases and
records of changed definitions, not only the current labels.
